The University of Valencia completed an educational project called "Arte Valenciano II" during the 2025-2026 academic year, resulting in 32 new Wikipedia articles about Valencian cultural heritage. The 51 enrolled students created content covering sculptures, churches, industrial sites, and other local heritage assets, while contributing 113 original images to Wikimedia Commons.
