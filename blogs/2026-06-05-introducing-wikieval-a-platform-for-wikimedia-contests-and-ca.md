WikiEval is a newly renamed platform (formerly WikiContest Tool) that streamlines Wikimedia contest management by centralizing submission tracking, evaluation, scoring, and result publication in one place. The tool supports flexible contest designs with multiple scoring systems, role-based access, and real-time dashboards while reducing manual administrative work for organizers, participants, and jury members.
