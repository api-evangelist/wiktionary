The United Kingdom held its first Wiki Science Competition since 2019, with User:Julian Herzog winning first place for a black-headed gull photograph. The organizer reflects on how mentorship and networking through the Wikimedia Youth Conference enabled the competition's success, while identifying future strategies like expanded outreach, social media promotion, and recruiting campus ambassadors to increase impact for the 2027 edition.
