Wikimedia Spain concluded its 2026 African Month initiative in May with the creation of 91 new articles on Spanish Wikipedia about African topics, contributed by five editors. This themed editing campaign aims to expand and improve coverage of the African continent on Wikipedia through collaborative community efforts.
