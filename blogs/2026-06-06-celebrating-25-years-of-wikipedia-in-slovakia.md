The Wikimedians of Slovakia held a celebration on June 6, 2026, commemorating 25 years of Wikipedia in the country. The event at Bratislava City Library brought together 15 active and former editors for networking, quizzes, presentations on Wikipedia's history, and a photo exhibition featuring images from community competitions like Wiki Loves Monuments.
