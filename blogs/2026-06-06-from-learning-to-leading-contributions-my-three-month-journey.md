Nabunje Leticia describes her three-month journey through the On Wiki Skills Program organized by Africa Wiki Women, where she developed practical editing skills across Wikipedia, Wikidata, and Wikimedia Commons. She successfully created articles documenting notable Ugandan individuals and contributed structured data, demonstrating how mentorship and community support transformed her from a learner into a confident Wikimedia contributor.
