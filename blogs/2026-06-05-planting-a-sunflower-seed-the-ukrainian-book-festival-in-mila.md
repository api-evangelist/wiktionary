On May 31, 2026, Wikimedia Italia organized the first Ukrainian-language edit-a-thon in Italy at a Ukrainian Book Festival in Milan, collaborating with the local Ukrainian community association. The event aimed to build Ukrainian Wikimedian communities in Italy and demonstrate that Wikimedia projects in Ukrainian could thrive even outside Ukraine, recognizing the importance of preserving Ukrainian language and culture among diaspora communities.
